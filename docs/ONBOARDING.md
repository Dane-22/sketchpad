## 1. Step 1: Account Registration
To get started, you must first create an account on our platform:
1. Navigate to our live environment: **[http://72.62.254.60:9000/](http://72.62.254.60:9000/)**
2. Click on the **Sign Up** or **Register** button.
3. Fill out your details, including a valid email address and your name.
4. Submit the registration form. 

*Note: You will not be able to log in immediately. Read Step 2 below.*

## 2. Step 2: Account Approval
Because our platform contains sensitive architectural blueprints and engineering data, all new accounts are placed in a **PENDING** state upon creation.
- A system Administrator will be notified of your registration.
- Once they verify your identity, they will approve your account.
- You will be able to log in to the system only after this approval has taken place. 
- If you urgently need access, please contact your project manager.

## 3. Step 3: Understanding Your Role
When your account is approved, the Administrator will assign you a specific Role. Your role determines what you can do inside the platform:
- **ARCHITECT / ENGINEER:** You have elevated permissions to draw on the canvas, manage files, and create new projects.
- **CLIENT / VIEWER:** You have standard access to view projects, leave comments, and chat with the team, but your drawing capabilities may be limited to prevent accidental modifications to final plans.

## 4. Step 4: Accessing Your First Project
Once approved, log in with your credentials to see your **Dashboard**:
1. **Recent Activity:** Check the activity feed to see what your team has been working on.
2. **Projects:** Click into a Project Folder assigned to you.
3. **The Canvas:** Open the project to enter the real-time collaboration canvas.

**Where to go next?**
- If you are a standard user or client, please read our **[Non-Techy Manual](./NON-TECHY-MANUAL.md)** to learn how to navigate the canvas and chat.
- If you are a technical drafter, please read our **[Tech-Aware Manual](./TECH-AWARE-MANUAL.md)** for advanced CAD shortcuts and import/export operations.

## 5. Help & Support
If your account is stuck in "Pending" for more than 24 hours, or if you experience any bugs while testing the system, please reach out to the Lead Developer (Daniel Rillera) directly for assistance.
